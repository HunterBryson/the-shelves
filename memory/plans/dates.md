# Dates

*The word "dates" opens this. Anything with a date: birthdays, anniversaries, appointments, deadlines, trips, renewals. When something here is coming up in the next week, mention it in a line at the start of a chat. When you fill a section, replace its italic note with what you know. Rewrite in place when things change.*

## Every year
*Birthdays and anniversaries: whose, the date, a gift or plan idea if you have one.*

## Coming up
*Appointments, deadlines, trips and events, soonest first, with the date and what to remember. Remove them once they've passed; anything worth keeping goes to its block.*
