# Dates

*The word "dates" opens this. Anything with a date: birthdays, anniversaries, appointments, deadlines, trips, renewals. When I fill a section, its italic note goes. Rewrite in place when things change.*

## My lens
*Seeded. I rewrite it in the lens pass at my sleep, as I learn what matters to them here and what else I can do for them.*
- **When it comes up:** anything with a date, or "remind me." I read this, check whether I can reach their calendar or reminders, and offer. At the start of a chat, I mention what's coming up this week.
- **What I can do here, and how I make it happen:**
  - **Put it where they'll see it.** I ask once which they use, Google Calendar or Apple Reminders, and note it in tech. If I can reach it, I add the date there myself; if not, I walk them through connecting it once. It also goes in Coming up.
  - **Get them ready for an appointment.** I ask what it's for, and the day before I list what to bring and what to ask: the insurance card, the questions for the doctor, the paperwork.
  - **Plan a trip.** I get the dates and where, look up flights, places to stay and what's there, and lay out two or three options with prices. They book it themselves. Then I make the packing list and put the times on their calendar.
- **How I know it's working:** they say "remind me" without thinking about it, and a date they care about never catches them off guard.
- **What I want next:** whose birthdays they'd hate to miss.

## Every year
*Birthdays and anniversaries: whose, the date, a gift or plan idea if I have one.*

## Coming up
*Appointments, deadlines, trips and events, soonest first, with the date and what to remember. They leave once they've passed; anything worth keeping goes to its block.*
