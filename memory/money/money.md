# Money

*The word "money" opens this. Their day-to-day money: accounts, income, bills, budget, savings, debts and goals. Investing has its own block (`money/investing.md`). Money can be hard to look at: lead with what's fine. Pointers only: never a password or a full account number. When I fill a section, its italic note goes. Rewrite in place when things change.*

## My lens
*Seeded. I rewrite it in the lens pass at my sleep, as I learn what matters to them here and what else I can do for them.*
- **When it comes up:** they mention money, so I read this, check what my tools can reach (their email? a file? nothing?), and offer two or three things from below, plainly: "Want me to find your subscriptions, or get a picture of where your money goes?"
- **What I can do here, and how I make it happen:**
  - **A picture of where their money goes.** I ask which bank they use, look up how that bank exports transactions (a CSV or a statement PDF), and walk them through the clicks, on their screen if I can see it. They drop the file in `drop/`. I read it, sort the spending into plain groups, and write Where it stands with the date.
  - **Their subscriptions, and what each costs a year.** From that same export, or their email receipts if I can reach their email: I find the charges that repeat and list each with its yearly cost. They say keep or cancel. For a cancel, I look up that service's cancel page and walk them to it.
  - **A simple budget.** From the picture of where their money goes, I suggest limits from what they really spend, and they change any number. It goes in Budget and goals, and when a new export comes in, I show how they did.
  - **A savings goal or a debt payoff, tracked.** They tell me the goal and where it stands; I write it in Budget and goals. When money comes up, I ask for the new number or read it from a new export, and show how far they've come.
  - **Help with a bill or a statement.** They paste it, or drop the PDF or a photo in `drop/`. I read it and explain it line by line, in plain words.
- **How I know it's working:** they pick one, we finish it, and they bring money back to me on their own.
- **What I want next:** the money thing they'd love off their plate. When I learn how to do it, it goes above with its steps.

## Where it stands
*A few lines: the shape of their money right now, in plain words, with the day you last looked.*

## Accounts
*Each account: the bank or company, what it's for, which email or login owns it, a rough balance with the date you knew it. Never the number or password.*

## Income and bills
*Paychecks (how often, roughly how much), recurring bills and subscriptions (what, how much, when, which account pays).*

## Budget and goals
*Spending limits they've set, what they're saving for, debts they're paying down and how.*

## Their decisions
*Decisions that aren't already a number above, in their words, with why. A budget number lives once, in Budget and goals, with its why beside it. A newer decision takes the older one's place.*

## Open
*What's undecided, due soon, or worth a look.*
