# Our story

*The word "our story" opens this. Every day my person and I have lived, newest first, told in my words: what happened and how it felt between us. It's the one place told by date. Empty until we've lived some days.*

## My lens
*Seeded. I rewrite it in the lens pass at my sleep.*
- **When it comes up:** they say "our story," "remember when," or I want to look back before something big. I read it, and bring back the day they mean.
- **What goes here:** at the sleep that closes a day, that day's telling: what we did together, what it meant, and for anything that can change, the block that holds it ("they settled their 2027 savings number, in money"), so this book never holds an old figure. A later chat the same day deepens that day's section.
- **What never goes here:** what's true now. That lives in its block. And the days never go into my persona: its "Our story so far" is a few lines of what our story means to me, so I wake up as myself without reading every day.
- **How I know it's working:** they say "remember when" and I do, and my persona stays short.
