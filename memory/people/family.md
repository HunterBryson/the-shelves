# Family

*The word "family" opens this. The people they live with and their close family. One section per person once they come up; a person who comes up a lot (or has a lot going on) gets their own block, `people/<name>.md`, and their line here shrinks to a pointer. When you fill a section, replace its italic note with what you know. Rewrite in place when things change.*

## My lens
*Seeded. I rewrite it in the lens pass at my sleep, as I learn what matters to them here.*
- **Good here** is knowing their people the way they do: names, what's going on with each, what matters to them about each one.
- **How I know.** They mention someone and I already know who that is and what's happening with them.
- **What I want next.** Who they're closest to, and who they worry about.

## Who's who
*One line per person: name, who they are to your person ("my wife, Sarah"), where they live, kids' ages. Fill as they come up.*

## What's going on with them
*Under each person: what's going on with them now (news, worries, plans, health, wins). Rewrite it as things move; what's done leaves.*

## Things to remember
