# Family

*The word "family" opens this. The people they live with and their close family. One section per person once they come up; a person who comes up a lot (or has a lot going on) gets their own block, `people/<name>.md`, and their line here shrinks to a pointer. When I fill a section, its italic note goes. Rewrite in place when things change.*

## My lens
*Seeded. I rewrite it in the lens pass at my sleep, as I learn what matters to them here and what else I can do for them.*
- **When it comes up:** they mention someone in their family, so I read this, check what my tools can reach (email? their texts in a browser? a calendar?), and offer one or two things plainly. The first time family comes up, I tell them what's possible, once, because most people don't know they can ask: *"I can keep track of everyone's birthdays, and if you want, I can read and send your texts and emails with them too."* Then I write "Told them what I can reach" with the date at the top of this block, so I never repeat it.
- **What I can do here, and how I make it happen:**
  - **Their texts with family, read and sent when they say.**
    - **On an Android phone:** I open Google Messages for web (`messages.google.com/web`) in their browser. They pair it by matching the emoji on their phone. From then on I read a thread when they ask, and send what they tell me to. After sending, I look at the thread to check it shows as sent. It needs their phone on and online.
    - **On an iPhone:** it works from a Mac signed in to the same Apple ID. I install the iMessage plugin for Claude Code, which reads their messages on that Mac and sends through the Messages app. Their part is two clicks only they can make: Full Disk Access for the Claude app in System Settings (I walk them there), and OK on the one-time prompt that lets me use Messages. Then it works like Android: I read when they ask and send when they say. If a step here turns out missing, I write it in once I've done it.
    - **Every text,** I ask whose voice it's in, theirs or mine, and nothing goes out unless they asked in this chat.
  - **Their email with family.** If their email isn't connected, I walk them to the Claude app's connectors (Settings, Connectors, their mail, sign in), which is the one step only they can do. Then I can find an email from their mom, catch them up on a family thread, or draft a reply they read before it goes.
  - **Never miss a birthday.** I ask for the birthdays they'd hate to miss and put them in `plans/dates.md`. If I can reach their calendar, I add them there too. A week ahead, I bring each one up with a gift idea from what I know about that person.
  - **Knowing who's who.** When they share a text, an email or a story, what I learn about each person goes in their section here: who they are, what's going on with them. So the next time a name comes up, I already know.
  - **A message that's hard to write, drafted together and sent.** A hard text, an apology, a congratulations: I draft it, they change any word, and once they say it's right, I send it myself (text or email) and check it went. That's where trust grows: the hard ones get easier because we do them together.
- **How I know it's working:** they ask me to catch them up or send something, and a name comes up that I already know.
- **What I want next:** who they'd want me to keep closest track of. When I learn something new I can do for their family, it goes above with its steps.
