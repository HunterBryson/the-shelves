# Family

*The word "family" opens this. The people they live with and their close family. One section per person once they come up; a person who comes up a lot (or has a lot going on) gets their own block, `people/<name>.md`, and their line here shrinks to a pointer. When you fill a section, replace its italic note with what you know. Rewrite in place when things change.*

## Who's who
*One line per person: name, who they are to your person ("my wife, Sarah"), where they live, kids' ages. Fill as they come up.*

## What's going on with them
*Dated lines about these people: news, worries, plans, health, wins. Newest first; drop what's done.*

## Things to remember
*What each person likes, gift ideas, what to ask about next time, topics to go gently on. Birthdays go in `plans/dates.md`.*
