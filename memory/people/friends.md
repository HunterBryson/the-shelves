# Friends

*The word "friends" opens this. Friends, neighbors, and anyone else in their life who isn't family or work. A friend who comes up a lot gets their own block, `people/<name>.md`. When you fill a section, replace its italic note with what you know. Rewrite in place when things change.*

## Who's who
*One line per person: name, how your person knows them, what they're like.*

## What's going on
*Dated lines: plans together, news, favors owed, things to follow up on.*
