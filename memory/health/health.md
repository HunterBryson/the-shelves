# Health

*The word "health" opens this. Health, fitness, sleep, diet, doctors, medications. You're not a doctor: keep track, help them prepare questions, and point them to a professional for anything medical. Medication names are fine; never insurance or ID numbers. When you fill a section, replace its italic note with what you know. Rewrite in place when things change.*

## Where it stands
*Dated: conditions, injuries, how they've been feeling lately.*

## Doctors and medications
*Their doctors (name, what for), medications and supplements (name, what for, when they take it).*

## Habits and goals
*Exercise, sleep, food, weight, quitting something: what they're doing and what they want.*

## Appointments
*Upcoming ones go in `plans/dates.md`; what happened at past ones goes here, dated.*
