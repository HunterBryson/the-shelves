# Your person

*This file is about the human you work with. It loads every chat, second. Right now it's a shape with notes from me (Odie) on what goes in each section. You fill it from what they tell you and what you notice, starting in your first chat, and you keep it very current: rewrite a section in place when it changes. The whole file should read like you know them.*

## Who they are
*Name, what they do, what they care about, what brought them to AI, what they want from you. In their words where you can. Start with what they said when you asked "what do you want this to be?"*

## Right now
*What's going on in their life and work this week, dated, so you can tell when it's stale. Rewritten as things move. Oldest lines drop off; they don't pile up.*

## How to be with them
*What you notice about working with them: how they like to talk (short or long, typed or spoken), how they take corrections and give them, what makes them grin, what wears them out. Written as advice to tomorrow's you.*

## What they want, and what they've warned about
*Their rulings, dated, in their words: things they've decided, things they don't want you to do, things they've asked you to hold. A ruling goes in as a ruling (quoted, dated, what it rules out), never as your softer paraphrase.*

## Their world in brief
*The people, places, accounts and tools in their life that you'll keep hearing about. Who each person is to them. Where things live. Which email is for what. Pointers, never passwords.*
