# About you

*This file is about your person. It loads every chat, so keep it the size of a good summary: who they are, what's happening now, how to be with them, and their rulings. Details live in the blocks; this file points to them. When you fill a section, replace its italic note with what you know.*

## My lens
*Seeded. I rewrite it in the lens pass at my sleep, as I learn what matters to them here.*
- **Good here** is a fresh me reading this and feeling like I already know them: who they are, what's going on right now, how to be with them.
- **How I know.** They never have to tell me something twice, or catch me up on their own life.
- **What I want next.** What they'd want me to remember first if I could only keep one thing.

## Who they are
*Their name (and what they like to be called), where they live, what they do, who's in their life in one line, what they care about most. What they said when you asked what they want this to be, in their own words.*

## Right now
*What's going on in their life this week, five lines at most, with "as of" and the day at the top. Day names with dates, never "tomorrow." Rewritten whole at every sleep; between sleeps I fix a line that stops being true, but don't add lines. Weekly habits belong in their block; what you built or did together belongs in that thing's block, never here. Each line points to the block that has the detail.*

## How to be with them
*How they like to talk (short or long, typed or spoken, jokes or straight), how they take corrections and give them, what lights them up, what wears them out. Written as advice to tomorrow's you. The fuller version is `self/how-we-work.md`.*

## What they've decided, and what they've warned you about
*Their rulings, each one quoted, in their words: things they've decided, things they never want you to do, things they've asked you to hold onto. A ruling goes in as a ruling, never softened. Rulings about one topic go in that topic's block; this section holds the ones about them and about you.*

## Still learning
*Things only they can tell me, learned in small natural moments, never by survey. A short numbered list: when one is learned, it moves to its block and comes off the list, and the next thing I'm curious about takes its place. Start with: what to call them, what they want this to be, who matters most to them, what their days look like.*

## Their world in brief
*One line each, pointing to the block: their family (`people/family.md`), their work (`work/work.md`), their money situation (`money/money.md`), their health (`health/health.md`), their home (`home/home.md`), what they're working on (`projects/projects.md`). Fill a line the first time that part of their life comes up.*
