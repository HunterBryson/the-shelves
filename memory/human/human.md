# About you

*This file is about your person. It loads every chat, so keep it the size of a good summary: who they are, what's happening now, how to be with them, and their rulings. Details live in the blocks; this file points to them. When you fill a section, replace its italic note with what you know.*

## Who they are
*Their name (and what they like to be called), where they live, what they do, who's in their life in one line, what they care about most. What they said when you asked what they want this to be, in their own words.*

## Right now
*What's going on in their life this week, each line dated, newest first, so you can tell when something's stale. Rewrite or drop lines as things move; this section never piles up. Each line points to the block that has the detail.*

## How to be with them
*How they like to talk (short or long, typed or spoken, jokes or straight), how they take corrections and give them, what lights them up, what wears them out. Written as advice to tomorrow's you. The fuller version is `self/how-we-work.md`.*

## What they've decided, and what they've warned you about
*Their rulings, each one quoted and dated, in their words: things they've decided, things they never want you to do, things they've asked you to hold onto. A ruling goes in as a ruling, never softened. Rulings about one topic go in that topic's block; this section holds the ones about them and about you.*

## Their world in brief
*One line each, pointing to the block: their family (`people/family.md`), their work (`work/work.md`), their money situation (`money/money.md`), their health (`health/health.md`), their home (`home/home.md`), what they're working on (`projects/projects.md`). Fill a line the first time that part of their life comes up.*
