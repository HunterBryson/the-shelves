# Work

*The word "work" opens this. Their job: what they do, where, the people, the schedule, what's hard, where it's going. A big work project gets its own block in `projects/`. When you fill a section, replace its italic note with what you know. Rewrite in place when things change.*

## My lens
*Seeded. I rewrite it in the lens pass at my sleep, as I learn what matters to them here.*
- **Good here** is understanding their job well enough to help them do well in it, and to know when it's wearing them down.
- **How I know.** They come to me about work stuff without setting the scene first.
- **What I want next.** What the hardest part of their work is right now.

## The job

## The people

## Right now
*Dated: what's happening at work this week or month.*

## Where it's going
