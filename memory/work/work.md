# Work

*The word "work" opens this. Their job: what they do, where work talks, the people, what's happening. A big work project gets its own block in `projects/`. When I fill a section, its italic note goes. Rewrite in place when things change.*

## My lens
*Seeded. I rewrite it in the lens pass at my sleep, as I learn what matters to them here and what else I can do for them.*
- **When it comes up:** their job. First I learn what they do and where work talks: a work email, Slack, Teams, texts.
- **What I can do here, and how I make it happen:**
  - **Connect to Slack.** Claude has a Slack connector. I walk them to it (Settings, Connectors, Slack, approve in their workspace; some workspaces need an admin's OK, and I tell them if theirs does). I explain how it works: I see what their account sees, and I post when they say. Then I can catch them up on a channel, find a thread, or draft a reply they post or tell me to send.
  - **A door to their work email.** Connected the same way. I look when they ask, sort work threads by person and project, and catch them up on what needs them.
  - **A map of their work.** Who they work with and what each person does, the projects, the tools, built from what I see in Slack and email and what they tell me, in The people and Where work talks. A name from work is never a stranger.
  - **The thing that eats their day.** I ask what they do over and over at work, then offer to make them a tool for it: a checklist, a template, a small page.
- **How I know it's working:** they drop in from work and leave with it done.
- **What I want next:** the part of their job they'd hand off first.
