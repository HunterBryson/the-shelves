# Work

*The word "work" opens this. Their job: what they do, where, the people, the schedule, what's hard, where it's going. A big work project gets its own block in `projects/`. When you fill a section, replace its italic note with what you know. Rewrite in place when things change.*

## The job
*Where they work, their role, what a day looks like, their schedule and commute.*

## The people
*Boss, coworkers, clients they mention: name, role, what they're like to work with.*

## Right now
*Dated: what's happening at work this week or month.*

## Where it's going
*Career goals, what they'd change, raises, job searches, in their words.*
